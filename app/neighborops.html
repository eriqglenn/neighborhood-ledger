    Letters & certificates

    Create formal Greater Fifth Ward Super Neighborhood 55 proclamations, certificates of recognition and president letters of support. Documents remain editable, traceable and clearly identified as community-issued rather than City of Houston proclamations.

    ⚖
    Authority and attribution

    Use the issuing organization and signatory fields accurately. A Super Neighborhood document should not use the City seal or state that it is an official City of Houston proclamation unless separately authorized through the appropriate City process.

    Before signature, verify names, dates, titles, factual claims, dollar amounts and the authority of the issuing organization. Letters supporting grants should not promise funding, zoning approval, City action or organizational commitments that have not been formally approved.
